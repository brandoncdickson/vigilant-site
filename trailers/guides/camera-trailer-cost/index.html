How much does a construction camera trailer cost to lease?

A construction camera trailer lease is priced per month. The price depends mainly on how long you need it, how many trailers you need, and what level of monitoring you want. Vigilant quotes a single monthly price that includes delivery, setup, maintenance and pickup.

What affects the monthly price

  • Lease length. Longer leases usually cost less per month.
  • Number of trailers. Large or long sites may need more than one.
  • Monitoring level. Live 24/7 monitoring with talk-down costs more than alerts sent only to your phone.
  • Analytics. Features like license plate recognition can add to the price.
  • Moves. Relocating a trailer as the job changes phases may carry a small fee.

What's included with Vigilant

  • Delivery, placement and setup
  • Cellular data, so you don't need site internet
  • Maintenance and repairs during the lease
  • Pickup at the end of the job
