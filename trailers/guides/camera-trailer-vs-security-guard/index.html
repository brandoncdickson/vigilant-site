Camera trailer vs. security guard for a construction site

A camera trailer watches the whole site all night with AI detection, records everything and costs less per month than a full-time guard. A guard can physically respond and check doors, but covers one spot at a time and can't record. Many sites use a trailer alone, and some pair a trailer with a guard or patrol.

Last updated November 12, 2026

Where a camera trailer is stronger

  • Covers a wide area from one mast, day and night
  • Records video and plate numbers you can hand to police or insurers
  • Never takes breaks, changes shifts or falls asleep
  • No site power or guard shack needed

Where a guard is stronger

  • Can physically check gates, doors and equipment
  • Can handle visitors and deliveries in person
  • Can respond to things a camera can't see, like inside a building

Which to choose

For most open construction sites, a trailer gives better coverage for the money. If you need someone to control access during the day or check inside a structure, add a guard or a patrol service alongside the trailer.
