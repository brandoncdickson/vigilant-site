Should you lease or buy a security camera trailer?

Leasing makes sense for most contractors because jobs end, needs change, and the lease covers maintenance and repairs. Buying only pays off if you'll keep trailers busy on site after site for years and can handle the upkeep yourself. Vigilant leases trailers only.

Why most contractors lease

  • No large upfront cost
  • Maintenance, repairs and batteries are our problem, not yours
  • Add or return trailers as jobs start and finish
  • You always get current equipment

When buying can make sense

  • You run many long jobs back to back and will keep trailers deployed nearly all the time
  • You have staff to maintain, move and repair them
  • You want to manage your own monitoring
