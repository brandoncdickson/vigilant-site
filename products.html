Vigilant-built mobile and solar products

Mobile Surveillance Unit

A solar-powered, cellular surveillance trailer with three 4MP cameras for 360° coverage, a PTZ camera with 25x zoom, a 120-decibel speaker, and police-style strobe lights for deterrence. Mounted on a 22 ft mast, it stores up to 30 days of footage and runs 5 to 7 days without sunlight. Live video and recordings are available in any web browser, smartphone, or tablet, with optional licensed monitoring. Tamper-resistant design with built-in GPS tracking.

  • Mobile surveillance
  • Solar
  • Cellular

Wall-Mounted Security System

Cellular-connected fixed-site security with a PTZ HD camera that zooms, spins, and tilts to cover large or distant areas. Three or four high-resolution cameras provide a 180° view, and license plate readers are available as an option. Built-in storage, a 120-decibel speaker, and strobe lights round out the system, with live view and playback on any browser, smartphone, or tablet.

  • Cellular
  • License plate readers (optional)

4G Solar-Powered Security Camera Kit

A wireless 4G camera kit for sites with no wired infrastructure. AI analytics detect human and vehicle movement and send smart alerts. Up to 9 days of battery life, an energy-saving mode for low-sunlight conditions, and a rugged IP67 waterproof design that handles extreme weather. Ideal for construction sites, remote areas, parking lots, farms and ranches, and critical infrastructure.

A trailer-mounted solar lighting tower for large areas and continuous operation. Two 50 W LED heads deliver 18,000 lumens on a 22 ft telescoping mast. A large solar and LFP battery bank provides multi-night runtime, with dusk-to-dawn automation and no supervision needed. Vision-ready mounts support cameras and PoE connectivity. Fuel-free and quiet, for construction perimeters, solar farms, access points, and work zones.

Guardian Beacon MLU

A compact, all-in-one solar lighting unit for areas trailers cannot reach. Silent and emission-free, with 12 V DC output for a camera mount. Suited to walkways, tool containers, interior paths, and sensitive work areas.
