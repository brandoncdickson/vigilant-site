How monitoring works

From camera alert to a call to your team

  1. AI checks every alert

    Our AI reviews the picture and live video from the camera and sorts the alert: Priority, Needs review, or Routine. Routine activity is logged for you, not called in.

  2. An operator takes it

    Real alarms go to a Vigilant operator. We aim to pick up every alarm within 60 seconds, and your monthly report shows how quickly we did.

  3. We call your people

    We call the contacts on your call list, in the order you choose, and keep going down the list until someone answers.

  4. You get the report

    After the call you get an incident report with the pictures, the video and what we saw, in your email and in the app.

You stay in control

Set it up the way your site runs

Managers change these from Settings in the app, any time, without calling us.

  • Call list. Who we call and in what order.
  • Monitoring hours. Dusk to dawn as standard. Add hours such as after closing, weekends and holidays, or choose 24/7 coverage for an added cost. Camera trailers include 24/7.
  • Pause. On site late? Pause monitoring for up to 12 hours and it turns back on by itself.
  • Ignore zones. Cover parts of a camera's view, like a public street, so the AI ignores activity there. Your recordings aren't changed.
  • Team logins. Invite your staff as Managers or Viewers. Viewers can watch cameras and read reports but can't change settings.

Evidence

Evidence your insurer and the police can trust

Managers can download an evidence package for any incident: the report, video, pictures, the AI's review, and a log of who viewed it.

Every file carries a fingerprint, so anyone can confirm nothing was changed after the fact.

Account security

Locked down like a security company should be

Sign in with Face ID, Touch ID or your Android fingerprint, or with your password plus a code from an authenticator app. Changing settings or downloading evidence asks you to confirm it's you. Your face never leaves your phone.

Settings lists every phone and computer signed in to your login, and if a phone is lost you can sign out every other device at once. When Vigilant support looks at your account to help you, they can only look, never change anything, and their visit shows in that list too.

Get the app

Installed in under a minute

  1. iPhone

    Open the customer login in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Turn on notifications in Settings.

  2. Android

    Open the customer login in Chrome and tap Install when it's offered. Turn on notifications in Settings.

  3. Computer

    Sign in from any browser. Everything in the app works on a computer too.
