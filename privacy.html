Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Vigilant Integrated Security LLC ("Vigilant," "we," "us") provides security cameras, monitoring and a customer portal at portal.vigilantintegratedsecurity.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number and site address when you become a customer or sign in to the portal.
  • Camera and security data: video, snapshots, motion and alert events, and device status from cameras we install or that you connect to the portal.
  • Usage information: basic logs such as sign-in times and the pages and reports you access in the portal.

Google Nest devices

If you choose to connect a Google Nest account, you sign in with Google and approve access through Google's own consent screen. With your permission we access, through Google's Smart Device Management API, the Nest devices and homes you select, including device names and status, camera events (such as motion, person and doorbell events), and live or event images and video.

We use this data only to show your cameras and events in your Vigilant portal, to deliver the alerts and monitoring you have asked us to provide, and to create incident reports for you. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except service providers that help us run the portal or as required by law.

Vigilant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Nest at any time from the portal or from your Google Account's partner connections (Google Home app → Settings → Works with Google / Partner connections). When you disconnect, we stop accessing your Nest devices and delete the stored access tokens.

How we use information

  • To install, operate, monitor and support your security system.
  • To provide the customer portal, alerts and incident reports.
  • To communicate with you about your service, billing and support.
  • To protect the security of our systems and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that host or support our systems under confidentiality obligations, with law enforcement or emergency responders when you request it or when required by law, or as part of a business transfer.

Retention and security

We keep camera footage and events only as long as needed to provide the service or as agreed in your service terms, then delete them. Account records are kept while you are a customer and as required for legal and accounting purposes. We use encryption in transit, access controls and limited staff access to protect your information.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or disconnect any linked account, by contacting us below.
